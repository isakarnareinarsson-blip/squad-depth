        How scoring works

        Every correct answer is counted. Your score depends on how often yours had been picked before you: the most-picked answer scores 1 point, and the rarer yours is next to it, the closer you get to 100. An answer picked by fewer than 1 in 100 players is a and scores the full 100. Two wrong guesses on a question and you're sent off with nothing. The daily challenge is the same five questions for everyone, and each can be answered once.
